FAQ

Common questions

Are you a CPA?

No. I provide bookkeeping services. I don't prepare tax returns or perform audits. I work alongside your CPA or tax preparer, and clean books usually make their job faster and cheaper for you.

I only closed a few deals this year. Is this worth it?

Maybe not every month. If your volume is low, a one-time Year-End Cleanup before tax season may be all you need. I'll tell you honestly on our call which option makes sense.

Can you tell me how much to set aside for taxes?

I'll give you an accurate profit number every quarter, which is what you and your CPA need to figure out estimated tax payments. Your CPA sets the actual amount, and I'm happy to send the numbers straight to them.

Do I need QuickBooks?

I work in QuickBooks Online or Xero. If you already use one, you just invite me as your accountant. If you don't have either yet, I'll help you pick one and set it up.

Is my financial information safe?

Yes. You give me accountant access inside your software. I never ask for your bank passwords, and I don't move money or pay bills on your behalf. Everything we share goes through secure folders, not email attachments.

My books are a year (or more) behind. Is that too far?

Not at all. That's what the Catch-Up Cleanup is for. Bigger cleanups cost more because they take longer, but you'll get a fixed price up front after our call.

Is there a contract?

Monthly plans are month to month. You can cancel with 30 days' notice. We'll sign a short one-page agreement that spells out exactly what's included.

What do you need from me each month?

Usually about 10 minutes: answering a short list of questions about transactions I can't identify, and uploading any receipts or invoices I ask for.

Do you only work with Pennsylvania agents?

Pennsylvania is my focus, but everything is done online, so I can work with agents and teams anywhere in the U.S.
