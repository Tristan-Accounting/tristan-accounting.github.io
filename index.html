FAQ

Common questions

Something else on your mind? Ask on our call or send me an email.

Are you a CPA?

No. Tristan Phillips Accounting provides bookkeeping services. I don't prepare tax returns or perform audits. I work alongside your CPA or tax preparer, and clean books usually make their work faster and less expensive for you.

Can you tell me how much to set aside for taxes?

Each quarter I send an accurate profit summary, which is what you and your CPA need to calculate estimated tax payments. Your CPA sets the amount, and I'm happy to send the numbers to them directly.

I only closed a few deals this year. Is this worth it?

If your volume is low, a one-time Year-End Cleanup before tax season may be all you need. I'll tell you honestly on our call which option makes sense.

Do I need QuickBooks?

I work in QuickBooks Online and Xero. If you already use one, you add me as your accountant. If not, I'll help you choose one and set it up.

Is my financial information secure?

Yes. You give me accountant access inside your software, and I never ask for your bank passwords. I don't move money or pay bills on your behalf, and documents are shared through secure folders rather than email attachments.

Is there a contract?

Monthly plans are month-to-month and can be cancelled with 30 days' notice. We sign a one-page agreement that spells out exactly what's included.

Do you only work with Pennsylvania agents?

Pennsylvania is my focus, but all work is done online, so I can work with agents and teams anywhere in the U.S.
